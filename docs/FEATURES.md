# Gameplay and features

Nightwatch follows one complete museum heist investigation from arrival to resolution. The case supports one investigator or a private team of two.

## Mission Control

Create a room, join using a private invitation, or resume an account-owned save. New-case creation asks whether to resume an existing investigation first. Each save offers personal archive deletion with confirmation and Undo. Deletion preserves the shared case and the partner's archive.

## Investigate

Explore four museum locations, collect ten authored evidence files, and compare three suspect accounts. The host controls when a waiting room starts. Shared discoveries become available to both investigators. Interviews, chronology, and activity remain accessible without crowding the main location view.

## Caseboard

Arrange cards by dragging their headers. Draw a connection from a card handle to another clue or suspect. Label it Supports, Contradicts, or Possibly related, then add an optional explanation. Select a card to read its original file, mark it reviewed or important, and attach a note.

Positions and connections are shared. Pan and zoom are personal. Reservations, revision checks, and conflict-aware Undo protect newer work. Fit board and Tidy layout help recover an arrangement. Smaller screens start in list view with tap-to-connect; keyboard arrows can move focused cards.

## Conclusion

Select a suspect, method, motive, and supporting clues. Explain the connections. The server validates the evidence and the conclusion. A two-person team must submit matching conclusions with sufficient support. Wrong theories do not disclose the answer.

A solved case unlocks the complete explanation, recovery, reconstructed timeline, proof, and contributions. Investigators may instead agree to reveal the solution. The outcome is recorded distinctly, and closed cases preserve their history.

## Voice, assistance, and export

Each investigator receives a short narrated opening for each new case. Completed briefings do not interrupt a resumed case. A compact replay button plays only the voice.

The optional evidence assistant selects verified excerpts from shared clues. It never sees unearned evidence or the hidden solution. Closed-case reports can be exported to PDF, including evidence, annotated connections, contextual notes, and recorded conclusions.
